# Start here: thinking like a computer

*Six short lessons for young beginners, about five minutes each, played with cards, paper and a partner. No computer needed. When you finish, the [Topics](../topics/index.md) and the [Books](../books/index.md) take you further.*

| | Lesson | You will |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [What is an algorithm?](01-What-Is-an-Algorithm.md) | write exact steps, and find out how exact they must be |
| 2 | [Sorting cards](02-Sorting-Cards.md) | sort a hand of cards two ways and count the work |
| 3 | [Guess my number](03-Guess-My-Number.md) | find a number from 1 to 100 in seven guesses |
| 4 | [Finding the way](04-Finding-the-Way.md) | find the shortest path through a maze by spreading out in rings |
| 5 | [Repeat until done](05-Repeat-Until-Done.md) | use loops and decisions, the two building blocks of every program |
| 6 | [How a computer learns](06-How-a-Computer-Learns.md) | teach a "computer" to tell apples from lemons with examples |

For teachers: every lesson is "unplugged" and works with a deck of cards, paper and pairs of students.
